# Macht — Training Framework v1

**Updated:** 2026-06-03  
**Purpose:** Define the training logic Macht should use while rebuilding fitness around the current shoulder injury.

---

## Premise

Macht is not a bodybuilding tracker.

It is a return-to-capability system.

The goal is to rebuild strength, conditioning, movement confidence, and injury resilience while respecting the current left shoulder injury.

Current injury anchor:

- Left shoulder anteroinferior labral tear, 3–6 o'clock
- No rotator cuff tear
- No Hill-Sachs lesion
- No bony Bankart lesion
- Mild supraspinatus tendinosis

Training should prioritize safe consistency over maximal loading.

---

## Current Training Rule

The shoulder should be treated as an active `avoid` injury until cleared by a clinician.

Macht should protect against movements that place the shoulder in risky positions, especially:

- Arm away from body plus external rotation
- Heavy overhead loading
- Hanging traction
- Dips
- Wide pressing
- Wrestling posting
- Sudden pulling or traction on the arm
- Heavy anterior shoulder loading

Any exercise that causes slipping, shifting, clunking, apprehension, sharp pain, numbness, tingling, or radiating symptoms should be stopped and marked as adapted.

## Default Training Split

Macht should default to Upper-Lower, not PPL.

Reason:

- PPL overemphasizes upper-body volume
- Upper-Lower adapts better around injury
- Lower-body training and conditioning can carry consistency while the shoulder recovers

Default weekly target:

- Minimum: 2 sessions
- Target: 3 sessions
- Strong week: 4 sessions

## Progression Rules

Progress only when all are true:

- Pain during session stays 0–2/10
- No instability sensation
- No apprehension
- No next-day flare
- No numbness, tingling, or radiating symptoms
- Movement quality remains controlled

If symptoms occur:

1. Reduce range
2. Reduce load
3. Substitute exercise
4. Mark session as adapted

## App Behaviour

When active injury is present:

- Hide red-tagged exercises
- Warn on caution-tagged exercises
- Suggest lower-risk substitutes
- Pause e1RM tracking for affected upper-body lifts
- Prioritize lower-body, conditioning, and rehab templates
- Mark adapted sessions without penalty
- Count minimum sessions toward consistency

When injury is cleared:

- Prompt 60% deload re-entry
- Keep caution warnings for 4–6 weeks
- Resume e1RM tracking only after stable completion of re-entry sessions
